PwC Cyber Security Consulting Virtual Experience
Overview

This repository documents my completion of the PwC Cyber Security Consulting Virtual Experience through Forage, a practical consulting simulation focused on governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) within a healthcare organization preparing for an Initial Public Offering (IPO).

The engagement simulated the work performed by cybersecurity consultants supporting a client through risk assessment, Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) review, IT General Controls (ITGC) testing, and executive reporting. Throughout the engagement, I evaluated internal controls, identified control deficiencies, analyzed supporting evidence, and developed remediation recommendations aligned with governance and compliance best practices.

Business Scenario

MedTech Industries, a healthcare startup preparing for an IPO, engaged PwC to evaluate its internal control environment before becoming subject to Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) compliance requirements.

The consulting engagement focused on identifying risks, evaluating IT and business controls, testing control effectiveness, and communicating findings to management.

Engagement Objectives
Assess business process and IT control risks
Evaluate Procure-to-Pay (P2P) controls for SOX readiness
Compare an SDLC process against NIST leading practices
Perform Test of Design (ToD) and Operating Effectiveness (OE) over IT General Controls
Identify control deficiencies and business risks
Present recommendations to executive leadership
Work Completed
Task 1 – Risk Assessment

Performed a risk assessment of the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process by reviewing documented procedures, identifying business process and system control gaps, and communicating findings through a consulting-style client email.

Skills Demonstrated

Business process analysis
Risk identification
SOX readiness assessment
Client communication
Task 2 – SDLC Walkthrough

Reviewed the client's Software Development Lifecycle against NIST guidance, identified missing controls, prepared walkthrough questions, and participated in a simulated client interview to validate the control environment.

Skills Demonstrated

SDLC review
NIST framework analysis
Audit walkthroughs
Client interviewing
Task 3 – IT General Controls Testing

Performed Test of Design (ToD) and Operating Effectiveness (OE) over change management controls by evaluating supporting evidence, identifying segregation of duties conflicts and emergency change approval deficiencies, and documenting findings using consulting workpapers.

Skills Demonstrated

Change Management
Segregation of Duties
Evidence inspection
Audit documentation
Task 4 – Executive Reporting

Prepared a management summary presentation communicating control deficiencies, business risks, and remediation recommendations for executive stakeholders.

Key Takeaways

This engagement reinforced the importance of evaluating controls through evidence rather than assumption, distinguishing between Test of Design and Operating Effectiveness, and communicating technical findings in a manner appropriate for executive stakeholders.
